Welcome to Threshold

As your coach at Threshold, I’m here to accommodate every body type, every experience level, and every type of learner. We take a brain-based approach to Fitness and Health Coaching. We work from the inside out, focusing on cranial nerve function, proprioception, and how your nervous system responds to physical and emotional tasks. This approach allows us to truly accommodate all people, including those with disabilities and chronic pain. As a Health Coach, my goal is not to make you replicate my health routine, but instead to give you the tools you need to be able to understand your health, advocate for yourself, and guide you to the life that YOU want to live. -Anna Bolton founder/CEO Why Health Coaching? As your coach, I’m here as an un-biased voice to help you assess your health, figure out attainable goals, teach you how to advocate for yourself, and what questions you should be asking when making decisions about your health. I’ll also work with you to find what remedies will help you raise your physical and mental thresholds and teach you how, and when to incorporate them in your day-to-day life. Why Movement? While the movement I teach is rooted in the traditional Pilates method, my teaching approach is influenced by Neuroanatomy. Which means instead of cueing your body, I cue your brain. Often, fitness professionals will try to treat muscle and joint pain/dysfunction without assessing the “Why?” behind it. It’s like putting the cart before the horse! Our nervousystem controls all movement and all bodily functions. By assessing the nervousystem first I’m able to get a more clear picture of what needs to be addressed, which leads to faster results, and equips you with the knowledge you need to be able to move with confidence.
