As your coach at Threshold, I’m here to accommodate every body type, every experience level, and every type of learner. I take a brain-based approach to Fitness and Health Coaching. why Threshold?

Why Health Coaching?

As your coach, I’m here as an un-biased voice to help you assess your health, figure out attainable goals, teach you how to advocate for yourself, and what questions you should be asking when making decisions about your health. I’ll also work with you to find what remedies will help you raise your physical and mental thresholds and teach you how, and when to incorporate them in your day-to-day life.

Why Movement?

While the movement I teach is rooted in the traditional Pilates method, my teaching approach is influenced by Neuroanatomy. Which means instead of cueing your body, I cue your brain. Often, fitness professionals will try to treat muscle and joint pain/dysfunction without assessing the “Why?” behind it. It’s like putting the cart before the horse! Our nervousystem controls all movement and all bodily functions. By assessing the nervousystem first I’m able to get a more clear picture of what needs to be addressed, which leads to faster results, and equips you with the knowledge you need to be able to move with confidence.
